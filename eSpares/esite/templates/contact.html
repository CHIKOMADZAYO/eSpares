Frequently asked questions

When will my preferred repair date be confirmed?

The date is a request. The team will review availability and update your repair record.

When will I know the repair cost?

A technician adds a quotation after reviewing the repair. You can approve or reject it from your account.

Can I book a collection?

You can request collection in the form. The team must confirm the address and availability before collection is arranged.

How do I follow progress?

Sign in and open your repair history, or use the high-entropy reference from your booking confirmation.

How do payments work?

Online payments are not enabled yet. Contact support for the current payment process; no card details should be sent through this form.
